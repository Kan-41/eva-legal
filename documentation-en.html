Documentation

Everything you need to know to use Eva, whether you're a member, staff or administrator.

🎟️ Opening a ticket (members)

1

Go to the server's ticket panel channel.

2

Pick a category from the dropdown menu (or click the matching button).

3

Fill in the form that opens (the reason is required, 10 characters minimum).

4

A private channel is created for you: only you and the staff can see it. You also receive a DM with the direct link.

When your ticket is closed, Eva asks you in DM for a 1 to 5 ⭐ rating.

🛠️ Managing a ticket (staff)

ButtonEffect
🙋 Claim / ↩️ UnclaimTake (or release) responsibility for the ticket.
✏️ RenameChange the ticket channel's name.
➕ / ➖ MemberAdd or remove someone from the ticket (by Discord ID).
🔒 CloseCloses the ticket: transcript archived, review request sent. The author can also close their own ticket.
🔓 ReopenReopens a closed ticket.
📄 TranscriptGenerates an HTML file of the conversation and sends it to the logs.
🗑️ DeleteArchives the transcript then deletes the channel.

Staff commands

/note ajouter — internal note invisible to the ticket author.
/note liste — shows the ticket's notes.

⚙️ Configuring Eva (administrators)

1

Log in to the Dashboard with Discord and pick your server.

2

Categories: ticket types (name, emoji, color, staff roles, custom form with up to 5 fields).

3

Panels: one or more panels (menu or buttons, live counter).

4

Logs: one channel per event type.

5

Settings: language, colors, welcome DM, reviews, auto-close, manager roles.

6

Type /panel in the desired channel to publish the panel.

❓ FAQ

I can't open a second ticket

Only one open ticket per category per person.

Staff aren't notified

Check the category's allowed roles in the Dashboard.

My ticket closed by itself

The server enabled automatic closing of inactive tickets.

I didn't receive the review DM

Your DMs are probably closed for this server.

Where are the transcripts?

In the logs channel (HTML file) and in the Dashboard, Tickets tab.
