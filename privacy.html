Privacy Policy

1. Stored data

Eva only stores the data required to operate: Discord identifiers (servers, channels, roles, users), ticket configurations, ticket content (reason, form answers, internal notes, reviews) and aggregated statistics. No data is sold or shared with third parties.

2. Purpose

This data is used exclusively to provide the service: ticket management, Dashboard, transcripts, statistics and reviews.

3. Retention

Data is kept as long as Eva is on the server, until deleted by an administrator or the bot is removed.

4. Deletion

An administrator can request full deletion of their server's data via the support server.

5. Cookies

The Dashboard only uses a secure session cookie (httpOnly, SameSite) required for authentication. No advertising cookies.

6. Discord OAuth2

Login via Discord OAuth2 (identify and guilds scopes). Eva never accesses your email, private messages or password. Access tokens are not stored.

7. Security

Encrypted sessions, CSRF protection, security headers, rate limiting, strict input validation and permission checks on every request.

8. Contact

Eva support server.
